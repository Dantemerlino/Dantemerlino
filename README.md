### About

Head and neck surgical oncologist and physician&ndash;scientist. My clinical practice covers the
surgical management of head and neck malignancy, including transoral robotic surgery and
microvascular free tissue reconstruction.

My research runs along two tracks. The first is applied AI and clinical informatics &mdash; evaluating
where language models are and are not reliable in surgical care, and building the data
infrastructure that makes outcomes measurable. The second is HPV-mediated disease, from
point-of-care vaccination in the otolaryngology clinic to prospective study of associated pathology.
Earlier, my PhD work mapped the GUCY2C gut&ndash;brain endocrine axis in appetite regulation and
neuronal injury.

<sub>24 peer-reviewed publications (12 first or co-first author) &middot; 2 book chapters, incl.
<i>Cummings Otolaryngology</i> 8e &middot; ~$137K in awarded research funding, incl. an NIH F30</sub>

### Clinical and Research Focus

- **Clinical:** Surgical management of head and neck cancer; transoral robotic surgery; microvascular free tissue transfer
- **Research:** HPV-mediated oropharyngeal carcinoma &mdash; vaccination, prevention, and associated pathology; surgical quality and safety
- **Informatics:** Clinical data pipelines, outcomes registries, and evaluation of language models in surgical care
